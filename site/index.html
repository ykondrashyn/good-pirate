Getting underway

Use a recent browser with WebGL 2. Hold and drag on the sea to steer, then release to stop accelerating. Follow the tutorial and use the on-screen controls for your fleet, map and island actions. The game keeps its portrait layout.

The first load downloads the game and may take a while. Tap or click the game to enable audio. Fullscreen is available in the player.

Your progress stays with you

The game saves locally in your browser. In Settings, use Copy Save to keep a backup or move progress to another browser. Use Paste Save to import it and confirm the replacement. Invalid saves are rejected.

Progress from the localhost version does not appear here automatically. Copy it from that version and paste it here. Private browsing and clearing site storage can remove local progress.
